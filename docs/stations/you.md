# You (PI) station

**Job:** approve protocols, sign claims, choose the next cut. You are the company CEO and the scientific signer.

## Gates you own

| Flag / action | When |
|---------------|------|
| `--approve-run` | New REINVENT / sampling preset |
| `--approve-dock` | New receptor / box |
| `--approve-literature` | New PubMed pull |
| MD / carb approvals | New length or protocol pack |
| Shortlist sign-off | “Worth synthesize / buy” |
| Outward summary | Anything leaving the lab |

## Decision surface (Portfolio)

Cross-modal shortlist (`output/portfolio/shortlist.csv`) and one-page campaign cards are the Decision MVP on the company roadmap — still human-weighted presets, not auto-CRM.

Until that lands: read Critic HTML, docking/MD tables, and campaign notes under [Campaigns](../campaigns/index.md).

## What you never delegate to a model

- Writing `reinvent.toml` or full mdp
- Inventing targets, boxes, or citations
- Declaring a wet-lab recommendation
